Help & FAQ

How do I upgrade my account tier?

To upgrade from Tier 1 to Tier 3, you need to provide your BVN and a valid government-issued ID (NIN or Passport). Visit the verification portal to begin.

Why was I charged a maintenance fee?

NairaFlow does not charge monthly maintenance fees. If you see a debit, it is likely a CBN-mandated stamp duty charge on transfers above ₦10,000.

How do I reset my transaction PIN?

Go to Security > Reset PIN. You will need to answer your security questions and enter an OTP sent to your registered phone number.

Danger Zone

Once you delete your account, there is no going back. All balances must be transferred out first.
